Article 3 – Transparent Entities

Reservation

Pursuant to Article 3(5)(a) of the Convention, the Republic of Panama reserves the right for the entirety
of Article 3 not to apply to its Covered Tax Agreements.


Article 4 – Dual Resident Entities

Pursuant to Article 4(3)(a) of the Convention, the Republic of Panama reserves the right for the entirety
of Article 4 not to apply to its Covered Tax Agreements.


Article 5 – Application of Methods for Elimination of Double Taxation

Reservation

Pursuant to Article 5(8) of the Convention, the Republic of Panama reserves the right for the entirety of
Article 5 not to apply with respect to all of its Covered Tax Agreements.
