Article 3 – Transparent Entities

Reservation

Pursuant to Article 3(5)(a) of the Convention, the Czech Republic reserves the right for the entirety
of Article 3 not to apply to its Covered Tax Agreements.

Article 4 – Dual Resident Entities

Reservation

Pursuant to Article 4(3)(a) of the Convention, the Czech Republic reserves the right for the entirety
of Article 4 not to apply to its Covered Tax Agreements.

Article 5 – Application of Methods for Elimination of Double Taxation

Reservation

Pursuant to Article 5(8) of the Convention, the Czech Republic reserves the right for the entirety of
Article 5 not to apply with respect to all of its Covered Tax Agreements.

Article 17 – Corresponding Adjustments

Reservation

Pursuant to Article 17(3)(b) of the Convention, the Czech Republic reserves the right for the entirety
of Article 17 not to apply to its Covered Tax Agreements on the basis that in the absence of a
provision referred to in Article 17(2) in its Covered Tax Agreement:

      i)      it shall make the appropriate adjustment referred to in Article 17(1); or

      ii)     its competent authority shall endeavour to resolve the case under the provisions of a
              Covered Tax Agreement relating to mutual agreement procedure.
