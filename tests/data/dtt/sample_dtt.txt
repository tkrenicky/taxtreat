Article 10
Dividends may also be taxed in the Contracting State...
The tax shall not exceed 5% if the beneficial owner holds at least 10 per cent.
Otherwise the tax shall not exceed 15%.

Article 11
Interest arising in a Contracting State shall be taxable only in the other State.

Article 12
Royalties may be taxed in the source State, but the tax shall not exceed 5%.
